How results are ranked

  1. Coverage. Pages containing all of your words come first, then pages with fewer of them.
  2. Relevance. BM25 scoring rewards rare words and doesn’t let long pages win just by being long.
  3. Proximity. Pages where your words appear close together get a boost.

Wrap words in "quotes" to require an exact phrase. Common words like “the” and “is” are ignored.
